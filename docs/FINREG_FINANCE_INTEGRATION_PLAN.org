* Purpose

Bring the robustness and completeness of Finreg's financial module into
Cellen without losing Cellen's school-management behavior and data.

The target is a production-ready, AGT-compatible financial system in which a
school can issue, print, share, settle, correct, report, and audit fiscal
documents. School concepts such as pupils, guardians, enrolments, school
years, tuition, meals, transport, and recurring contracts must remain native
parts of the workflow.

* Current assessment

Cellen is not starting from zero. Its backend already contains a substantial
accounts-receivable and AGT-oriented foundation. The principal problem is that
many capabilities are incomplete, weakly validated, or not exposed through a
usable client workflow.

The complete Finreg-to-Cellen comparison is still pending because the Finreg
checkout was not available during the first audit. Before implementation,
make the repository readable at =~/projects/finreg= or another agreed path and
complete the field-by-field comparison described in this document.

** Existing Cellen capabilities

- School-scoped fiscal document series and sequential numbering.
- FT, FR, ND, NC, and RC document records.
- Invoice lines with quantity, price, discount, IVA, and exemption data.
- Billing-item/service catalogue and prices per school year.
- Recurring school contracts and bulk monthly invoicing.
- Full and partial payments with invoice allocations.
- Receipts, credit notes, reversals, guardian credits, and refunds.
- Cash sessions, payment plans, reminders, and payment references.
- Expense registration and basic financial reports.
- SAF-T XML export.
- Hash-chain fields and signature excerpts.
- Links to schools, children, guardians, enrolments, and school years.

** Principal client-facing gaps

- No printable invoice, receipt, credit-note, or debit-note document.
- No PDF rendering or document download endpoint.
- No print, download, share, or email actions for fiscal documents.
- Invoice creation is hard-coded to =Mensalidade= and =Outras taxas=.
- The invoice form does not consume the existing billing-item catalogue.
- Users cannot add, remove, reorder, or edit arbitrary invoice lines.
- Receipt and credit-note screens expose only summary information.
- Billing items are services rather than a complete product and inventory
  domain.
- No supplier, purchasing, inventory, warehouse, or cost-of-goods workflow.
- No complete general ledger, chart of accounts, journal, accounts payable,
  bank reconciliation, or treasury subsystem.
- SAF-T and fiscal signing require comparison with Finreg and validation
  against current AGT requirements.
- Fiscal immutability and numbering concurrency require stronger database
  guarantees and tests.

* Integration principles

1. Do not replace Cellen finance wholesale.
2. Import or adapt Finreg's generic financial core beneath Cellen's school
   billing layer.
3. Preserve existing Cellen data, identifiers, document numbers, hashes, and
   audit history.
4. Retain =school_id= isolation on every financial entity and query.
5. Treat issued fiscal documents as immutable; use correction documents and
   reversals instead of destructive edits.
6. Use forward-only, reversible database migrations where practical.
7. Deliver complete vertical workflows rather than isolated screens or
   tables.
8. Validate AGT compliance with schemas, representative fiscal scenarios,
   and production-like exports.

* Domain mapping

| Cellen school concept          | Generic financial-core concept          |
|--------------------------------+------------------------------------------|
| School                         | Tenant / company                         |
| Guardian                       | Customer / billing party                 |
| Child                          | Beneficiary / document dimension         |
| School year                    | Business and reporting dimension         |
| Tuition, transport, meals      | Products or services                     |
| Uniforms, books, supplies      | Stocked products                         |
| School contract               | Recurring billing agreement              |
| Enrolment fee                  | Invoice source                           |
| Parent payment                 | Customer payment / receipt               |
| Scholarship or sibling benefit| Discount or pricing rule                 |

The generic customer must be the party responsible for payment, normally the
guardian. The child remains an optional beneficiary/reference on documents
and lines. A single guardian must be able to settle documents for multiple
children without losing per-child reporting.

* Required comparative audit

Before porting code, compare Finreg and Cellen in the following areas.

** Data model

- Fiscal documents, lines, series, numbering, hashes, and corrections.
- Customer and address master data.
- Product, service, tax, unit, price, stock, and warehouse data.
- Payments, allocations, receipts, cash, banks, and reconciliation.
- Suppliers, purchases, accounts payable, and withholding taxes.
- Chart of accounts, journals, posting rules, periods, and balances.
- SAF-T master files, source documents, and tax tables.
- Audit history, idempotency, tenant isolation, and soft deletion.

** Business workflows

- Draft, issue, print, email, settle, correct, cancel, and reprint.
- FT, FR, NC, ND, RC, pro-forma, quotation, and other supported documents.
- Partial payments, overpayments, credit use, refunds, and reversals.
- Cash opening, closing, variance, and approval.
- Purchasing, receiving, supplier invoicing, and supplier payments.
- Inventory movements and cost calculation.
- Accounting posting and period closing.
- AGT transmission, retries, rejection handling, and SAF-T export.

** Technical comparison deliverable

Produce a matrix for every relevant Finreg component with one of these
decisions:

- =REUSE=: can be copied with minimal adaptation.
- =ADAPT=: reusable after tenant or school integration changes.
- =MERGE=: both implementations contain behavior that must be preserved.
- =KEEP CELLEN=: school-specific implementation remains authoritative.
- =REBUILD=: neither implementation is safe or complete enough.
- =DEFER=: valuable but outside the first production milestone.

For each decision record source files, target files, schema changes,
dependencies, migration rules, tests, and risks.

* Delivery phases

** Phase 0: baseline and safety

*** Tasks

- Make the Finreg repository available for read-only comparison.
- Record current migration heads and production schema versions.
- Run the complete Cellen backend and Flutter test suites.
- Create representative anonymized finance fixtures.
- Inventory existing fiscal document numbers and hash chains.
- Document current API contracts used by the parent and administrator apps.
- Establish backup and rollback procedures for financial data migrations.
- Create the Finreg-to-Cellen comparison matrix.

*** Exit criteria

- Both repositories and schemas are understood.
- Existing school-specific behavior is explicitly catalogued.
- The migration path does not renumber or discard issued documents.
- Baseline tests and known failures are recorded.

** Phase 1: printable fiscal documents

This is the highest-priority client milestone.

*** Tasks

- Implement a server-side fiscal document rendering service.
- Produce professional A4 PDFs for FT, FR, ND, NC, and RC.
- Add optional receipt/thermal layouts where required.
- Add authenticated PDF preview and download endpoints.
- Add print, download, share, and email actions in the Flutter application.
- Allow parents to download their own invoices and receipts.
- Support safe reprints without issuing a new fiscal document.
- Mark copies/duplicates according to the applicable fiscal rules.

*** Required document content

- School/company legal name, NIF, address, contacts, and logo.
- Document type, number, series, issue date, and due date.
- Customer name, NIF, address, and final-consumer indication.
- Child/reference information without replacing the legal customer.
- Product/service code, description, quantity, unit, unit price, discount,
  tax rate, exemption reason, and line total.
- Net, tax, and gross totals.
- Payment method and settled-document references where applicable.
- Correction reason and original-document reference for NC and ND.
- Hash/signature excerpt and required AGT wording.
- Page numbering and document-copy indication.

*** Exit criteria

- An administrator can issue and print every supported fiscal document.
- A guardian can download their invoices and receipts.
- Rendered totals exactly match stored immutable totals.
- PDF golden tests cover taxable, exempt, discounted, partial-payment, and
  correction scenarios.

** Phase 2: complete invoice editor and catalogue integration

*** Tasks

- Replace the hard-coded tuition form with a dynamic line editor.
- Search and select catalogue products and services.
- Add, remove, reorder, and duplicate lines.
- Support quantity, unit, price, discount, IVA, and exemption reason.
- Inherit defaults from the selected product while allowing authorized
  overrides.
- Add customer/guardian selection and optional child association.
- Support FT, FR, and ND workflows with appropriate validation.
- Add document preview before irreversible issuance.
- Distinguish draft preparation from fiscal issuance.
- Validate totals on the server; never trust client calculations.

*** Exit criteria

- A user can issue an invoice containing any number of products or services.
- Catalogue price and tax settings populate automatically.
- Invalid tax/exemption combinations are rejected.
- School tuition remains fast to invoice through templates and contracts.

** Phase 3: robust Finreg financial core

Port or adapt the following after the comparative audit.

*** Fiscal document core

- Unified lifecycle and state transitions.
- Database-safe series allocation with concurrency protection.
- Immutable issued documents and controlled corrections.
- Customer and product snapshots on issued documents.
- Idempotent issuance and payment intake.
- Complete audit trails.

*** Receivables and treasury

- Customer accounts and statements.
- Payment allocation and unallocated credits.
- Cash sessions and cash movements.
- Bank accounts, deposits, transfers, and reconciliation.
- Payment reversal and refund approval flows.
- Ageing, collection, and delinquency reporting.

*** Accounting

- Chart of accounts.
- Double-entry journals and posting rules.
- Fiscal periods and closing controls.
- Customer, supplier, cash, bank, tax, revenue, and expense postings.
- Trial balance, general ledger, balance sheet, and income statement.

*** Purchasing and payables

- Supplier master data.
- Purchase requisitions and purchase orders where required.
- Goods/service receipt.
- Supplier invoices and credit notes.
- Accounts payable, due dates, payments, and withholding.

*** Products and inventory

- Product/service type distinction.
- Units of measure and price lists.
- Warehouses and stock movements.
- Purchase receipt, sale issue, adjustment, and return movements.
- Stock valuation and cost-of-goods integration.

*** Exit criteria

- Imported components respect Cellen tenant and authorization boundaries.
- Financial events generate balanced and traceable accounting entries.
- Existing Cellen receivables remain reconciled after migration.

** Phase 4: school-specific integration

*** Tasks

- Preserve recurring contracts for tuition, transport, meals, and activities.
- Connect enrolment fees to invoices and payment-dependent activation.
- Support prices per school year and school segment.
- Implement sibling, scholarship, staff, and campaign discounts.
- Support one guardian paying for multiple children.
- Add child and school-year dimensions to financial reporting.
- Connect meal orders, transport, uniforms, books, and supplies to products.
- Preserve parent-submitted payment proof and approval workflows.
- Add parent account statements, credits, payment references, and reminders.
- Keep school terminology configurable without changing fiscal semantics.

*** Exit criteria

- No existing school workflow is lost.
- Generic finance reports reconcile with school and child-level reports.
- Parent access remains restricted to their linked children and accounts.

** Phase 5: AGT compliance and production hardening

*** Tasks

- Compare signing, numbering, tax, and SAF-T behavior with Finreg.
- Validate SAF-T XML against the applicable schema.
- Validate customer, product, tax-table, invoice, payment, and correction
  exports.
- Test numbering under concurrent document issuance.
- Prevent backdating that breaks a fiscal series.
- Add database constraints or triggers for issued-document immutability.
- Implement AGT transmission queues, retries, and rejection handling when
  applicable.
- Add monitoring for numbering failures, rejected transmissions, unbalanced
  postings, and reconciliation differences.
- Perform authorization and tenant-isolation tests for every financial API.
- Conduct user acceptance testing with real school workflows.

*** Exit criteria

- SAF-T passes technical validation for representative production periods.
- Concurrent issuance cannot duplicate or corrupt document numbers.
- Issued fiscal data cannot be silently edited or deleted.
- Operational failures are observable and recoverable.

* First production milestone

Deliver this complete vertical workflow before attempting every accounting
feature:

#+BEGIN_EXAMPLE
Select guardian and child
        -> add catalogue products/services
        -> preview and issue invoice
        -> print/share invoice PDF
        -> record and allocate payment
        -> issue and print/share receipt
        -> issue credit/debit correction when required
#+END_EXAMPLE

This milestone directly addresses current client complaints and establishes
the document architecture required by later Finreg integration.

* Data migration rules

- Never renumber an existing issued document.
- Never recompute or silently replace an existing fiscal hash.
- Preserve original creation, issue, payment, reversal, and correction dates.
- Preserve invoice-to-payment allocations and credit balances.
- Map current billing items to products/services using stable identifiers.
- Snapshot legal customer and product data on issued documents.
- Reconcile counts and monetary totals before and after every migration.
- Use per-school migration reports with exceptions requiring manual review.
- Keep legacy identifiers or an explicit mapping table for traceability.
- Do not delete legacy tables until production reconciliation and retention
  requirements have been satisfied.

* Testing strategy

** Unit tests

- Monetary rounding and tax calculations.
- Discounts and exemption rules.
- Series allocation and fiscal hashes.
- Payment allocation, credits, refunds, and reversals.
- Accounting posting balance.
- School pricing and discount rules.

** Integration tests

- Invoice through receipt and reconciliation.
- Partial payment and multi-invoice payment.
- Overpayment and credit application.
- Full and partial credit note.
- Cash-session opening and closing.
- Product sale and stock movement.
- Supplier purchase and payment.
- SAF-T generation from representative data.

** End-to-end tests

- Administrator fiscal workflow on web and mobile.
- Parent invoice and receipt download.
- Recurring tuition bulk generation.
- Multi-child guardian statement.
- Role and tenant isolation.
- PDF print/share behavior.

** Migration tests

- Production-like database upgrade.
- Document counts and totals before and after migration.
- Hash and document-number preservation.
- Rollback or forward-fix rehearsal.

* Risks and mitigations

| Risk                                      | Mitigation                                      |
|-------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------|
| Loss of school-specific behavior          | Explicit mapping and regression tests           |
| Corruption of fiscal numbering            | Database locks, uniqueness, concurrency tests    |
| Historical data mismatch                  | Per-school reconciliation reports                |
| Incorrect AGT output                      | Schema validation and representative fixtures    |
| Duplicate concepts from both systems      | Component decision matrix before porting         |
| Excessive one-time merge                  | Phased vertical delivery                         |
| Client/server total disagreement          | Server-authoritative calculations                |
| Cross-school data exposure                | Mandatory tenant filters and isolation tests     |
| Issued-document mutation                  | Immutable states and database enforcement        |
| UI exposes incomplete backend operations  | End-to-end acceptance criteria per workflow      |

* Definition of done

The integration is complete when:

- Schools can create invoices containing arbitrary products and services.
- All fiscal documents can be previewed, printed, downloaded, shared, and
  safely reprinted.
- Guardians can access their invoices, receipts, statements, and credits.
- Payments, allocations, corrections, reversals, and refunds are fully
  traceable.
- School-specific contracts and enrolment workflows continue to work.
- Financial records are tenant-isolated and issued documents are immutable.
- Accounting entries are balanced and reconcile to operational documents.
- SAF-T exports pass the agreed AGT validation process.
- Existing Cellen financial history has been migrated without loss or
  renumbering.
- Automated tests cover the complete critical workflow.
