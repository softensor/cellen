* Verified Finreg findings

- FastAPI/PostgreSQL tenancy derives company context from JWT identity and
  applies it to PostgreSQL session context.
- Feature entitlements already gate billing, customers, products, reports,
  recurring invoices, POS, settings, and API-key capabilities.
- The backend already has arbitrary invoice lines, fiscal documents, payments,
  allocations, receipts, recurring invoices, PDFs, reports, SAF-T, audit,
  accounting, treasury, and inventory.
- Drafts support UUID =client_request_id= with a company-scoped uniqueness
  guard; recurring occurrences also have a uniqueness guard.
- Official PDFs use =GET /api/v1/documents/{document_id}/pdf=.
- Current JWT login is tied to a normal Finreg user/company; delegated Cellen
  integration identity is absent.
- Shared packages exist, but billing pages remain coupled to Finreg's global
  Riverpod API/auth/router and dynamic map models.
- External-system mappings and structured school document context are absent.
- =AGT_SANDBOX= is deployment-wide, so automated fiscal finalization is unsafe
  on a production-configured single backend.
- Finreg-side detail is in
  =/workspace/finreg/docs/CELLEN_SCHOOL_VERTICAL_SPEC.org=.
* Purpose

Integrate Finreg's complete sales and billing experience into Cellen while:

- keeping Finreg independent and reusable by many external applications;
- keeping Cellen's school-management behavior intact;
- making Finreg the sole authority for fiscal documents and balances;
- avoiding a fork of Finreg frontend and backend behavior;
- supporting future verticals such as schools, clinics, hospitality, retail,
  and professional services.

* Non-negotiable boundaries

** Finance user-interface ownership

The primary Cellen Finance route MUST render the released
=finreg_sales_ui= module. Cellen MUST NOT present its historical invoice,
receipt, credit-note, SAF-T, debtor, statement, audit, product, or cash-session
screens as an alternative operational fiscal workflow.

Cellen contributes school context and vertical views (pupils, guardians,
academic years, and student billing plans) through explicit Finreg host
adapters. Fiscal documents, corrections, receipts, tax calculation, numbering,
reporting, and official PDFs remain Finreg-owned operations. A legacy Cellen
finance view may exist only as a separated read-only archive and MUST NOT be
the default Finance entry point.

** Finreg owns

- Fiscal companies, customers, products/services, tax configuration, series,
  numbering, signing, hashes, rounding, and immutable document lifecycle.
- FT, FR, NC, ND, RC and other supported commercial documents.
- Payments, allocations, receipts, reversals, refunds, and customer credit.
- Official PDFs, fiscal copies, SAF-T, fiscal reports, and audit history.
- Its backend, database, releases, certification scope, and public API.

** Cellen owns

- Schools, pupils, guardians, guardian/pupil relationships, classes,
  enrolments, and academic years.
- Pupil enrolment, academic eligibility, school-year context, sibling or
  scholarship policy, and school operational notifications.
- The decision that a school event should create a charge.
- Authorization to see or initiate school-related financial activity.
- References linking a Finreg result to a pupil, enrolment, school year, and
  Cellen billing instruction.

** Cellen must not own for new documents

- Fiscal numbering, signing, hashes, tax calculation, official totals, PDFs,
  SAF-T, or fiscal reporting.
- An independent writable invoice/payment/receipt lifecycle.
- A fallback fiscal issuer when Finreg is unavailable.

* Platform layers

Finreg fiscal kernel (certified invariants)
        |
Finreg generic sales and billing backbone
        |
Finreg vertical profiles and extension API
        |
Reusable Finreg Flutter sales UI + client SDK
        |
Product connector/host adapter (Cellen, future systems)
#+END_EXAMPLE

** Fiscal kernel

Cannot be overridden by a vertical: numbering, taxes, signing, corrections,
immutability, payments, PDFs, SAF-T, and audit.

** Generic commercial backbone

Customers, catalogues, drafts, invoice lines, quotations where supported,
sales documents, settlement, statements, receivables, and sales reports.

** Vertical profile

A profile adds terminology, structured metadata, capability configuration,
workflow hooks, report filters, and optional UI contributions. It must not
fork fiscal behavior. Prefer data-driven configuration to code.

** Product connector

The school profile is generic; the Cellen connector understands Cellen IDs,
authorization, navigation, theme, guardian selection, pupil selection, and
synchronization. Another school system can implement a different connector
against the same school profile.

* School profile

** Concepts

| School concept                   | Finreg representation                  |
|----------------------------------+----------------------------------------|
| School legal entity              | Company/tenant                         |
| Guardian responsible for payment | Customer                               |
| Pupil                            | Beneficiary/external reference         |
| Academic year                    | Structured external dimension          |
| Enrolment                        | Structured source reference            |
| Tuition/transport/meals          | Product or service                     |
| Uniform/book/supplies            | Product, stocked if applicable         |
| Student billing plan             | Finreg recurring invoice template      |
| Signed school agreement          | Separate Cellen legal document         |
| Parent payment                   | Finreg payment and allocation          |

The guardian is normally the fiscal customer. A pupil must not silently
replace the legal customer. One guardian may pay charges for several pupils.

** Structured external context

Finreg needs a versioned extension field or metadata contract, subject to
inspection of its current model:

{

  "source_reference": "billing-instruction-id",

  "academic_year": {"external_id": "...", "label": "2026/2027"},

Only approved fields may appear on official documents. Finreg decides what is
fiscal content, printable reference, internal metadata, or report dimension.

* Student billing-plan model

The legacy Cellen =Contract= record is not a legal contract. It is a
single-line recurring-charge configuration and MUST NOT be presented to users
as an enforceable agreement. The product term is =Student Billing Plan=
(Portuguese: =Plano de Cobrança do Aluno=).

Finreg is authoritative for the plan, its multiple billable lines, price and
tax snapshots, recurrence, next run, pause/resume state, generated documents,
payments, and corrections. Cellen supplies and validates school context:
pupil, payer/guardian, enrolment, academic year, and eligibility changes.

A plan contains one payer, one pupil context, an effective period, recurrence,
due-date policy, and one or more Finreg product/price lines. Tuition, transport,
meals, and enrolment fees are lines of one plan; they are not separate legal
contracts. If the school needs a signed agreement, Cellen stores it separately
with parties, version, acceptance/signature, and effective dates, and may link
it to the Finreg plan.

There is exactly one recurrence executor: Finreg. Cellen creates, updates,
pauses, or resumes the authoritative Finreg template when enrolment or
eligibility changes. Cellen MUST NOT run a competing monthly invoice scheduler.

Price and IVA come from mapped Finreg products/prices. A deliberate override
requires a reason and effective date; document occurrences retain immutable
snapshots. Cellen catalogue and legacy contract data become migration inputs or
read-only projections after cutover, never a second commercial source of truth.

** Lifecycle

1. Cellen resolves the pupil and responsible guardian.
2. Cellen maps selected school services to Finreg products.
3. Finreg creates an idempotent, multi-line recurring template in draft mode.
4. Finreg's scheduler creates one occurrence per due period with duplicate
   protection.
5. Authorized fiscal mode may finalize that occurrence; shadow mode keeps it a
   non-fiscal draft.
6. Finreg events update Cellen projections and school workflows.
7. Withdrawal or ineligibility pauses the template; it never silently deletes
   issued history.

* Cellen behavior to preserve

** Retained school-domain capabilities

- Billing plans per pupil with an explicit responsible guardian.
- Fallback to the pupil's primary-contact guardian.
- Academic-year eligibility and school discount/waiver decisions.
- Start/end dates and enrolment-driven pause/resume decisions.
- Reference-month duplicate prevention, executed authoritatively by Finreg.
- Bulk-generation warnings for missing billing guardians.
- Regeneration after an earlier charge is legitimately cancelled.
- Parent payment-proof submission and school approval workflow where required.
- Payment plans, reminders, overdue school notifications, and Multicaixa
  references, after ownership comparison with Finreg.
- Enrolment activation only after confirmed settlement of its fee.
- Parent access constrained by guardian/pupil relationships.
- Per-pupil and per-academic-year operational reporting.

** Required refactoring

- Cellen manages a Finreg recurring template through an idempotent integration
  command; it does not create a parallel local recurring contract.
- Finreg confirmation updates a Cellen projection and mappings.
- Enrolment activation consumes a confirmed Finreg settlement event/status.
- Overdue notifications consume authoritative Finreg receivable state.
- Parent statements and fiscal balances come from Finreg.

* Frontend module architecture

Flutter mobile code cannot be downloaded dynamically. Reuse must use
compile-time packages plus runtime configuration.

** Proposed packages in Finreg

- =finreg_client_sdk=: typed API, authentication/session abstraction, errors,
  pagination, models, idempotency, and file streaming.
- =finreg_sales_ui=: generic catalogue, invoice editor, documents, payments,
  receipts, statements, PDFs, and reports.
- =finreg_vertical_school_ui=: optional pupil/guardian context widgets,
  terminology, school filters, and school metadata form.

Finreg and Cellen compile the same released packages. Cellen must not maintain
a copied long-lived fork.

** Runtime manifest

Finreg returns company capabilities and vertical configuration at runtime:

- vertical/profile identifier;
- enabled document types and modules;
- terminology and allowed school metadata;
- supported payment methods;
- tax/exemption choices;
- report availability;
- UI schema and API versions.

Runtime data changes behavior; it does not download executable Dart code.

* Integration topology

Recommended first topology:

#+BEGIN_EXAMPLE
Cellen Flutter -> Cellen integration gateway -> Finreg API/backend
#+END_EXAMPLE

The Cellen gateway is not a fiscal backend. It authenticates the Cellen user,
derives the school, resolves mappings, obtains a Finreg session, forwards the
request, streams documents, and records correlation/status data.

Direct Flutter-to-Finreg calls may be introduced later if Finreg supports
OAuth/token exchange, short-lived company-scoped tokens, CORS, and appropriate
mobile/web security.

* Identity and authorization

- Never embed long-lived Finreg credentials in Flutter.
- The browser cannot select an arbitrary Finreg company ID.
- Cellen derives the school from its authenticated tenant context.
- Finreg tokens are short-lived and scoped to company, actor, roles, and
  operations.
- Every cross-system request carries a correlation ID and actor identity.
- Access to a fiscal document requires both Finreg company authorization and
  the Cellen guardian/pupil relationship where applicable.
- Add company-bound integration clients with short-lived scoped tokens and
  delegated Cellen actor audit context. Native Finreg login remains unchanged.

* Mapping and integration data in Cellen

- School to Finreg company.
- Guardian to Finreg customer.
- Billing item to Finreg product/service.
- Cellen user/employee to Finreg actor when required.
- Billing instruction to Finreg draft/document.
- Payment proof/submission to Finreg payment.
- Finreg document/payment/receipt/correction IDs.
- Pupil, enrolment, academic-year, and billing-period context.
- Idempotency key, correlation ID, synchronization status, timestamps, and
  structured error code.
- Optional read-only projections for fast UI and offline-tolerant lists.

Projections must be refreshable and must never become an alternate ledger.

* API requirements

Finreg must expose or confirm support for:

- Company capability/vertical manifest.
- Scoped authentication or token exchange.
- Customer upsert by source and external ID.
- Product upsert/mapping or catalogue lookup.
- Draft creation/update and authoritative preview.
- Idempotent issue of FT/FR and retrieval/search.
- NC/ND correction referencing an original document.
- Idempotent payment registration/allocation and receipt retrieval.
- Official PDF streaming/download.
- Customer statements, outstanding/ageing, and sales reports.
- SAF-T operation/status appropriate to authorized Finreg users.
- Webhook/event feed or updated-since polling.
- Structured errors, pagination, API version, and correlation IDs.

Existing native endpoints must be reused through common application services.
Required additions are external-ID upserts, structured vertical context,
scoped integration authentication, uniform payment/receipt idempotency, and a
change feed or signed webhooks.


* Reliability

- Stable idempotency key per Cellen business action.
- Transactional outbox for asynchronous/bulk work.
- Safe retries after timeout, including timeout after Finreg committed.
- Authenticated, replay-safe, idempotent event consumption.
- Reconciliation by external ID, Finreg ID, status, and amount.
- Explicit pending, failed, rejected, and confirmed states.
- No local fiscal fallback during Finreg outage.

* Existing data transition

Each current Cellen finance component must be classified as:

- =SCHOOL=: retain;
- =GATEWAY=: replace with Finreg integration;
- =PROJECTION=: synchronize read-only;
- =MIGRATE=: import to Finreg if supported and legally correct;
- =ARCHIVE=: retain read-only in Cellen;
- =RETIRE=: disable after reconciliation.

Historical documents must never be silently renumbered, re-signed, or
reissued. No generic legacy-import contract was verified; final treatment
requires certification/accounting review and may use a read-only Cellen archive
plus an approved opening/cutover balance.
